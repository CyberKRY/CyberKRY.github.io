About

Who I Am

My interest in cybersecurity started at an early age. I have always been curious about how computers and systems work. At first, I experimented with web development and built simple websites using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Over time, I discovered penetration testing, and that is when I realized this was the field I wanted to pursue. Since then, I have been actively practicing on TryHackMe, solving challenges and working through vulnerable machines to build my practical skills.

My main area of interest is offensive security, especially web application security and bug bounty. I spend a lot of time studying web vulnerabilities, learning different attack techniques, and improving my understanding of how modern applications can be exploited. I am still learning and consider myself to be at a junior level, but I already have a solid practical foundation and continue to improve through hands-on practice and CTF competitions.

My goal is to become a strong web penetration tester and eventually earn the OSCE3 certification. Right now, I am focused on gaining as much practical experience as possible and continuously developing my offensive security skills.
