Who I Am

I'm a cybersecurity specialist with a passion for offensive security and ethical hacking. My journey started with curiosity about how systems work under the hood, which quickly evolved into hands-on practice on platforms like TryHackMe and HackTheBox.

I specialize in web application penetration testing, with a strong foundation in OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities. I actively complete PortSwigger Web Security Academy labs to sharpen my skills and document my findings in detailed write-ups.

Currently working toward my OSCP certification and looking to contribute to bug bounty programs and real-world security assessments.
